# GOAL-FACTOR-FAILURE-ATTRIBUTION-01

This research-only goal attributes why the existing 120 fixed candidates did
not pass readiness after the expanded-evidence rerun. It consumes existing
Rerun02 and Quant04 evidence only.

It reports criterion failures, old-to-new degradation, regime/sign
instability, exact existing-metric fingerprint redundancy, limited effective
cross-sectional breadth, provider fragility, and family-level continue,
redesign, or stop decisions.

It does not calculate new factor values, search formulas or thresholds,
reclassify a candidate as ready, fetch data, or create recommendation,
position, portfolio, dashboard, trading, production, local-lake, broker,
factor-mining, or DQN/RL outputs. GOAL-10D remains separately locked.
