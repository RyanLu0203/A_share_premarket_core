# GOAL-ALPHA-HYPOTHESIS-REDESIGN-01

This design-only goal freezes the five failed price-derived factor families
and pre-registers four economically distinct hypotheses: liquidity shock,
timestamped event drift, PIT fundamental revision, and sector-neutral
valuation/quality dispersion.

Every hypothesis is blocked pending a separate evidence-acceptance goal. The
preferred first design is liquidity-shock normalization, but construction is
not authorized until breadth reaches at least 100 symbols and volume,
turnover, free-float, trade-status, and PIT availability contracts pass.

No factor values, provider calls, threshold searches, evaluation rows, or
downstream outputs are created. V2 factor mining remains locked.
